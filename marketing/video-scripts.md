# Enlivonex: funny Hinglish promo scripts that invite DMs

Five shoot-ready vertical video scripts for Reels, Shorts, or TikTok. Each one
calls out a specific kind of creator, uses a light joke, shows real app footage,
and ends with one easy next step: DM **DEMO** for the beta details.

Keep the delivery conversational, like you're telling a creator friend—not
reading an ad. Aim for 20–30 seconds. Replace `[YOUR HANDLE]` before posting.

## The simple offer

> Enlivonex is a Windows desktop beta for an AI-assisted short-video workflow:
> start with a topic, create a draft using your configured options, then review
> the video on your PC. You decide whether to publish. Early tester access is
> currently planned at ₹100 one-time; explain exactly what's included, system
> requirements, and any provider costs before accepting payment.

## DM replies that keep the conversation useful

**When someone sends DEMO**

> Hey, thanks for reaching out! Enlivonex is a Windows desktop beta for an
> AI-assisted short-video workflow: create a draft from a topic, then review it
> on your PC. Want me to send the demo, system requirements, and beta details?

**When someone asks the price**

> Early tester access is planned at ₹100 one-time. Before any payment, I’ll
> share exactly what access includes, the Windows requirements, setup
> expectations, and any third-party AI provider costs or requirements. Want
> those details?

**When someone sends BETA**

> Thanks! It’s an early build, so I’m looking for people comfortable testing
> and sharing honest feedback. I’ll send the confirmed access scope and
> requirements first; you can decide after reviewing them.

Only send a demo, requirements, or terms you have actually confirmed. Don't
claim guaranteed views, revenue, virality, fully offline AI, or automatic
publishing. Never ask people to DM passwords, API keys, or Google sign-in codes.

## Posting and filming checklist

- Replace `[YOUR HANDLE]`; use the same DM keyword shown in that video's CTA.
- Put the key audience or hook in the first two seconds; add readable captions.
- Show genuine app screens and a real output you have permission to share.
- Keep provider names, API keys, OAuth codes, account emails, tokens, and private
  paths out of frame.
- Label sped-up footage; don't imply that a sped-up render is real-time.
- Keep the ₹100 offer and beta status legible. Confirm what the fee covers,
  requirements, contact channel, and access terms before taking payment.
- Don't imply all AI processing is offline. Configured providers may process
  requests through their own online services.
- YouTube connection and upload are optional and require user authorization and
  confirmation; never show a live upload as automatic.
