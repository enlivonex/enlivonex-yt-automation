Is Enlivonex a SaaS app?

No. The current product is a Windows desktop application. This site describes a desktop beta, not a cloud-hosted SaaS product.

How do I pay and get the download?

Sign in with a verified Gmail account and submit a request. If approved, we will send payment instructions and the QR code by email. We verify payment manually; only then will we issue a one-time code to that same Gmail address. Do not pay based on a QR code or payment request from anyone else.

Why do I need Google sign-in?

It verifies that you control a Gmail address and helps us identify the Google account to add as a YouTube API test user if needed. The website sign-in does not connect your YouTube account or grant the app YouTube permissions.

Does the access code protect the ZIP?

No. The code expires after 30 days and can be redeemed once for your verified Gmail account, but it reveals a public download link. Anyone who obtains that link can share or use it without signing in. The code does not activate or lock the installed app.

What information does the request form store?

Google verifies your Gmail account; we store the verified email address, selected PC type, request status, and access-code timestamps to administer the beta and manage tester access. We do not store your Google password or sign-in token. Records are retained until manually deleted by the beta administrator. Do not include passwords, API keys, payment PINs, or sign-in codes.

Does the app upload videos automatically?

No. You review the generated video, connect your YouTube account if you want to publish, and confirm the upload yourself.

Does all AI processing stay on my PC?

Not necessarily. Project files are handled locally, but processing depends on your selected AI provider. Cloud providers may receive prompts or other data needed for their service. Review each provider’s terms before use.

What should I expect as a tester?

This is early-access software for Windows. You may encounter issues or changing features, and we’ll ask for feedback. System requirements and support details will be shared before payment.
